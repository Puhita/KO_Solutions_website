About us

We are two friends who love making things on a computer. What started as late-night side projects turned into ko-solutions: the place where we put the games and tools we build together.

We are both developers from Sofia, Bulgaria. We write .NET and Angular by day, and in our spare time we team up on games and tools we want to exist.
